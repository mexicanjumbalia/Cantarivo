# Contributing to Cantarivo

Thank you for helping build a safer, consent-led singing companion.

## Source-code contributions

Submit only work that you wrote or have authority to contribute. By submitting a pull request, you license your contribution under the repository's current [PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0](LICENSE) for inclusion in the project. You keep ownership of your original contribution unless a separate signed agreement says otherwise.

This contribution rule is not a copyright assignment and does not give the project a commercial license to a contributor's work. Before accepting material contributions for a commercial product, maintainers should use a lawyer-reviewed contributor agreement or assignment and confirm the chain of title.

## Audio, lyrics, voice, and music are different

Do not submit music, lyrics, recordings, backing tracks, stems, samples, voice models, or artist likeness material in a pull request or issue. The public repository accepts only assets that satisfy the strict [CC0-only Open Content Policy](docs/OPEN_CONTENT_POLICY.md), and any future partner material follows the separate [Music Collaboration Framework](docs/MUSIC_COLLABORATION_FRAMEWORK.md).

Never submit material that imitates a real performer's voice or identity without explicit, specific written authority. Default: do not submit AI voice material.

## Privacy and safety

Do not include personal contact details, payment data, audio recordings, access tokens, or real driver data. Keep the app's driver-first defaults intact: local processing where possible, clear consent, low distraction, and an obvious manual off switch.
