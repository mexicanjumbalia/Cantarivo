# Music collaboration framework

**A public invitation and rights checklist - not a contract, offer, or legal advice.**

## The creative brief

Cantarivo is interested in original, consent-led vocal moments that can make singing feel more companionable. We welcome conversations with vocalists, writers, producers, and labels that can document the rights needed for an app experience.

For an initial collaboration discussion, contact [drivercompanionsuppteam1@gmail.com](mailto:drivercompanionsuppteam1@gmail.com). Please send a short rights summary first; do not send audio files before a rights path has been agreed in writing.

We will not accept "unlicensed" audio, chart-song covers, lyrics, backing tracks, stems, samples, or voice-clone materials on the promise that an artist said yes. A public streaming link, social post, or verbal permission is not enough. Do not send audio files until a rights path has been agreed in writing.

## Public-repository rule

This public repository remains **CC0-only** for audio, under the [Open Content Policy](OPEN_CONTENT_POLICY.md). A partner asset that is not expressly dedicated to CC0 for both the underlying composition and the sound recording must not be added to this repository, bundled into the public demo, or listed as available content.

A future commercial product may explore separately licensed original assets outside this repository only after a separate, signed agreement, rights review, and product decision. This framework does not authorize that future use.

## Rights checklist before any delivery

For every proposed asset, identify the people or entities with authority over each item below and obtain written permission or a signed agreement that covers the actual proposed app use.

| Right or question | Minimum written confirmation |
| --- | --- |
| Composition and lyrics | All songwriters/publishers, ownership splits, and authority to reproduce, adapt, distribute, and make the work available in the app. |
| Sound recording (master) | Performer/producer/label ownership and authority over the exact final recording, stems, and edits. |
| Performer and voice | Consent to use the performer's recorded vocal; separate, specific consent for name, image, likeness, marketing, synthetic-voice generation, training, or voice cloning. Default: no AI voice use. |
| Samples and third-party material | Written clearance for every sample, loop, stem, lyric, beat lease, library asset, session-player contribution, and co-writer contribution - or a documented confirmation that none exists. |
| App scope | Territory, term, platforms, download/stream/cache use, edits/adaptations/harmonies, marketing, analytics, takedown path, exclusivity, credits, and payment/royalty reporting. |
| Business assurances | Warranties of authority and originality, indemnity allocation, union/label/publisher obligations, governing law, and a signed rights holder. |

Musical compositions and sound recordings are separate works and can be owned by different parties. The U.S. Copyright Office explains that the recording is not a substitute for rights in the underlying composition. See [its overview](https://www.copyright.gov/register/pa-sr.html).

## Proposed collaboration path

1. **Short introduction, no files.** Describe the creative concept, contributors, and whether the work is wholly original.
2. **Rights map.** List writers, producer, performers, label/publisher, splits, samples, previous releases, and all required approvals.
3. **Business discussion.** Agree on intended app use, credit, compensation, term, territory, reporting, and takedown expectations.
4. **Written agreement.** Use a lawyer-reviewed agreement signed by the people or entities who actually control the necessary rights. Do not rely on a generic "work made for hire" label; legal ownership depends on the facts and signed writing. See the [Copyright Office's ownership guidance](https://www.copyright.gov/title17/92chap2.html).
5. **Secure delivery and release check.** Retain the signed agreement and a dated rights record outside the public repository. Add only public-safe metadata after the release conditions are satisfied.
6. **Ongoing control.** Honor the agreement's credit, payment, privacy, and takedown terms. Keep a kill switch for disputed assets.

Use the [Producer and Vocalist Collaboration Term Sheet](PRODUCER_VOCALIST_TERM_SHEET_TEMPLATE.md) to organize the early discussion. It is deliberately nonbinding; a final agreement needs legal review and signatures from the actual rightsholders.

## Deferred synchronized-vocal experience

Cantarivo may later offer an opt-in, rights-cleared listening mode that pairs a song's authorized master or backing track with the vocalist's own original raw vocal or stems. The goal is authentic, timing-aligned playback; it is not singer imitation, voice cloning, music recognition, or a substitute for a performer's consent.

This is deferred until after the initial controlled preview. Before implementation:

1. Use a written agreement that separately covers the song/master or backing track, raw vocal or stems, composition and lyrics, performer/voice consent, and every sample or session contribution.
2. Require delivery metadata: BPM, time signature, sample rate, channel layout, a fixed start offset or beat grid, and approved mix/stem versions.
3. Precompute timing locally and test drift and latency across phone speakers and Bluetooth/car audio. Do not use microphone capture of the user or passengers for alignment.
4. Let the user select the experience in a stationary controlled test environment; never auto-start it or offer timing/mix controls while moving. Keep an immediate stop/quiet control.
5. Keep raw vocal files local unless a future written agreement and privacy review specifically authorize a different path. Do not retain user or passenger audio.
6. Treat this as a production-audio and rights feature: it needs device testing, audio-focus/interrupt handling, accessibility review, and legal/store review before public release.

## Plain-language invitation copy

> Cantarivo is exploring original, rights-cleared vocal collaborations for an opt-in singing companion. We are looking for artists and producers who control, or can document authority for, both the song and the specific recording. We do not accept unlicensed material, covers, samples without clearance, or voice-clone content. Any use would begin only after a rights review and a written agreement that clearly covers the recording, composition, performer consent, credit, compensation, and opt-in app use.

No partner is asked to waive rights by participating in a conversation. Each collaboration should be reviewed independently by qualified counsel before any audio is delivered or used.
