# Donations and sponsorship

**Status: no payment destination is active. This policy is not tax, financial, or legal advice.**

## Current safe setup

The repository's Sponsor button leads only to the project's [support page](../donate.html). That page collects no payment details and has no active donation link. The static demo never receives card, bank, or tax information.

## Before activating donations

1. Choose a funding recipient that the project owner is legally able to control. For stronger public-identity separation, consider a project-owned organization only after checking the platform's identity, payout, tax, and disclosure requirements.
2. Use a provider's verified HTTPS destination. GitHub supports GitHub Sponsors, listed funding platforms, and up to four custom URLs through `.github/FUNDING.yml`. See [GitHub's funding-file guide](https://docs.github.com/en/repositories/managing-your-repositorys-settings-and-features/customizing-your-repository/displaying-a-sponsor-button-in-your-repository).
3. Publish the provider name, recipient type, permitted uses of funds, refund/chargeback contact route, and a plain-language privacy statement. Do not claim donations are tax-deductible unless the recipient is actually eligible and the statement is accurate.
4. Update `donation-config.js`, the support page, and this policy in the same release. Review every public link before enabling it.
5. Keep payment identity details out of the repository. Public repository ownership, a public sponsor profile, and a payment provider may still reveal information the project cannot safely hide. GitHub notes that public profiles and sponsorships can expose information, and sponsorships are generally not tax-deductible. See [GitHub's profile guidance](https://docs.github.com/en/account-and-profile/reference/profile-reference) and [sponsorship tax guidance](https://docs.github.com/en/sponsors/sponsoring-open-source-contributors/about-sponsorships-fees-and-taxes).

## Donation principles

- Support is optional and never changes the app's safety, consent, or privacy defaults.
- Donations do not purchase ownership, a music placement, product access, a commercial license, or special treatment.
- No sponsor data is collected by this static website. The chosen provider's own terms and privacy policy apply to payment processing.
- Maintain a simple public record of how support is used once funding becomes active, without publishing donor information unless the donor and provider settings make that appropriate.
- If the project becomes a business, hires contributors, or receives material funding, obtain tax and legal advice before accepting money.
