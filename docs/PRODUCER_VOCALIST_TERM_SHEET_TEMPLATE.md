## Nonbinding boundaries

Until a final agreement is signed by the people or entities that actually hold the required rights:

- no party grants a license, assignment, ownership interest, confidentiality obligation, exclusivity promise, or right to use an asset;
- no party must deliver audio, pay money, or proceed with a release;
- a verbal statement, email, social post, or upload does not replace a signed agreement; and
- the project must not add non-CC0 partner material to the public repository.

## Final-agreement checklist

The lawyer-reviewed agreement should identify, at minimum, the master recording; underlying composition and lyrics; all rightsholders; scope, term, and territory; fees/royalties and accounting; credits; edits and derivative work permissions; publicity and performer-consent terms; sample clearances; AI/voice restrictions; warranties and indemnity allocation; governing law; and a takedown process.
