Current Android preview answers

These answers apply only to the current binary: no account, no analytics or advertising SDK, no INTERNET permission, local AI vocal cues only, and microphone samples processed and discarded on the device. Re-check the final app bundle and every included SDK before submitting.

The Android build declares microphone and media-playback foreground-service types so a user-started singing session can continue under other apps with an ongoing notification. It does not request phone-state or call-log access. Calls are handled only as temporary Android audio interruptions, without caller or call metadata.

Play Console questionDraft answer for the current build

Audio files / voice or sound recordingsNot collected or shared. The microphone is used for ephemeral on-device level and signal processing; raw samples are not saved or transmitted.
Location, contacts, identifiers, messages, photos, files, app activity, or diagnosticsNot collected or shared.
Encryption in transit / deletion requestNot applicable to this build's in-app data. It does not transmit or retain user data and does not offer accounts.

Google defines collection for this form as transmitting data off the user's device. Processing that remains solely on-device and is never transmitted is not collection for this disclosure. This is not a substitute for reviewing the complete current policy. Google Play Data Safety guidance

Do not reuse these answers for a cloud AI singer

If a future AI singer sends microphone audio, a recording, a transcript, a prompt, an identifier, or telemetry to a provider, this table becomes inaccurate. The developer must update the privacy policy, prominent in-app disclosure, Data Safety answers, consent flow, supplier review, retention/deletion process, and security assessment before enabling that version.

At minimum, a cloud-audio design would require a new review of Audio files: voice or sound recordings; whether it is collected and shared; the exact provider; app-functionality purpose; encryption in transit; retention; deletion; and whether the feature is optional. Do not guess at those answers: obtain them from the signed provider terms and the exact SDK/model behavior.
