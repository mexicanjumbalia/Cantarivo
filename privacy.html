PRIVACY POLICY

Your voice stays
your business.

Effective date: August 2, 2026

Scope

This policy describes the Cantarivo website, concept demo, and any official Cantarivo app release that links to this page. It describes the project's intended data practices. It does not describe features that are not yet built.

Publisher identity

The public developer name is Cantarivo Labs. The app name is Cantarivo, and its planned store title is Cantarivo: Singing Companion. Cantarivo Labs LLC is the intended legal entity name, but it must not be represented as an active limited liability company until the formation state accepts its filing. Until then, “Cantarivo Labs” identifies the project team.

Audience and content rating

Cantarivo does not use an in-app adult age gate. The publisher must declare an accurate target audience and complete the applicable content-rating questionnaire in Google Play Console for each release. If future features introduce age-restricted content or child-directed design, the app and this policy must be reviewed before release.

What we do not collect or sell

Cantarivo does not sell, rent, trade, license, or share personal information with data brokers or third-party buyers. The current demo does not create user accounts, record or save microphone audio, create voiceprints, identify speakers, transcribe speech, identify songs, or upload microphone audio. A small CC0 music playtest catalog is stored locally in the app; it is not personal data and playback is not logged or sent anywhere.

We do not use microphone data or recognized commands for advertising, profiling, analytics, training, or sale.

During an explicitly enabled Android singing session, the app can calculate a temporary sound level and a limited possible vocal-like-moment signal on the device. That signal is not singing detection and cannot tell whether a sound came from the user, another person, music, or background noise. Raw samples, periodicity, and voice characteristics stay in memory and are discarded when listening stops. When the person has separately enabled wordless harmony or local AI vocal cues, a coarsely rounded current pitch can be passed in memory to the local interface only to set the tone; it is not stored, profiled, or transmitted.

Future voice controls

The Android app offers only a bounded, on-device command session after the user chooses it for the current singing session. It can listen for one of four commands: “Companion join,” “Companion quiet,” “End session,” or “Help.” The command listener stops after one command, when it times out, when the app is no longer visible, or when the user taps a stop control. Voice controls are off by default, visibly indicated while active, and immediately stoppable by a clear in-app control or device permission revocation.

Audio and recognized command text will not be recorded, stored, transmitted, sold, or used to identify a person. If a device cannot offer the approved on-device path, voice controls will be unavailable and the app will keep its button controls. Before any release changes these commitments, this policy, the app's permission explanation, and applicable store disclosures will be updated first.

Background sessions and interruptions

After a person starts a singing session while the app is visible and grants microphone access, the Android app can keep local sound analysis active while another app is visible. Android displays an ongoing foreground-service notice for the active session. That notice provides Pause or Resume and End session controls. The service is not started at boot and is not silently restarted from the background.

When Android reports an active call, communication mode, or a higher-priority microphone user, Cantarivo pauses or yields its microphone analysis and stops its own companion cue. The app can restore the same active session after the interruption if the person keeps the Resume after calls setting enabled; otherwise it waits for manual resume. Cantarivo does not request phone-state, call-log, contact, caller identity, or message access and does not learn who called.

AI vocal companion

The current app includes an optional local AI vocal cue prototype. It is a short, wordless, voice-shaped sound generated on the device after the user has allowed the companion for the current session and enabled local AI vocal cues. It is not a human singer, voice clone, cloud audio service, lyric generator, artist imitation, or claim that the app can sing a song.

Before Cantarivo enables prerecorded AI vocal packs, on-device model inference, or any cloud AI vocal feature, the exact feature, audio/data flow, model or provider, performer and music rights, retention, and stop controls must be reviewed. If any audio or related data would leave the device, the app will require a new prominent in-app disclosure and consent immediately before activation, plus an updated policy and store declaration.

Microphone permission and your choices

The current demo asks for microphone access only after you choose to turn it on for a singing session and after a plain-language explanation of its purpose and background duration. You may decline, pause it from the notification, turn it off, end the session, disable automatic interruption resume, or revoke access in your device settings. After Android permission is granted, Cantarivo does not repeatedly ask unless permission is later revoked, app data is cleared, or the app is reinstalled. Declining will not prevent use of the button-first controls. Android may also ask once for notification permission so ongoing session controls can appear in the notification drawer.

Other people and lawful use

Voice features can hear nearby sound while they are active, even though this project's intended design does not save or transmit that sound. Do not enable voice controls around other people unless you have their permission and local law permits the use. Do not use Cantarivo to record, monitor, identify, or profile another person.

Website hosting, support, and platform services

This website is hosted through GitHub Pages. GitHub may process ordinary technical request data under its own privacy terms in order to host the site; Cantarivo does not add website analytics or a tracking SDK. If you choose to support the project, GitHub Sponsors handles that transaction under GitHub's own terms and privacy practices. Cantarivo does not receive payment-card or bank details.

If you email the project, we receive the information you choose to include and use it to respond to your message. Please do not send sensitive personal information, recordings, or transcripts by email unless necessary for your request.

Apple, Google, device manufacturers, mobile operating systems, and app stores operate their own services. Their handling of device-account information, permission prompts, system-model assets, store transactions, and platform telemetry is governed by their own notices. This policy describes the Cantarivo project's code and services, not those independent services.

Retention, security, and privacy requests

Because the current demo does not keep accounts, recordings, transcripts, or a user profile, there is no in-app audio or transcript archive to retrieve or delete. The project's privacy approach is data minimization: do not collect the data in the first place. No system can promise absolute security, and you should use the current demo only in a browser and environment you trust.

For a privacy question, correction, deletion request relating to a message you sent us, or a report that this policy does not match a release, contact drivercompanionsuppteam1@gmail.com.

Google Play Data Safety review

The current Android preview processes microphone samples only on the device and does not transmit or retain them. Our pre-submission Data Safety review sheet records the proposed current-build answers and the conditions that would require new answers. That page is a draft aid; the publisher must complete and submit accurate answers in Google Play Console for the exact release.

Changes

We will update the effective date and this policy before making a material change to microphone, speech, analytics, advertising, account, or sharing practices. If a release begins storing or transmitting audio or transcripts, it will require a new policy review and a new user-facing disclosure before release.

Important note

This policy is a project privacy commitment, not legal advice and not a substitute for a lawyer's review of the jurisdictions where a public app is offered. It is written to match the current demo and the no-cloud, no-recording native design described in the project documentation.
